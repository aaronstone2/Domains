# References & Evidence Base

Every claim in this corpus is anchored to a graded evidence base. Sources are sorted into four tiers by methodological strength: **T0** denotes meta-analyses and systematic reviews (the highest-confidence syntheses of pooled data); **T1** denotes individual randomized controlled trials and controlled experimental studies (including EMG trials); **T2** denotes expert and textbook material (peer-reviewed narrative reviews, anatomy textbooks, and evidence-based practitioner syntheses such as Stronger by Science, Renaissance Periodization, MASS, and Menno Henselmans); and **T3** denotes mechanistic and practitioner sources (anatomy references, open exercise databases, and coaching material). The corpus drew on **169 ingested sources** across these tiers — 35 at T0, 32 at T1, 67 at T2, and 35 at T3 — grouped below by subdomain.
