Philosophy & schedule

RIR TARGETS (evidence-based): Heavy compounds (squat, deadlift, hinge, fixed-core press/pull) = 2-3 RIR most sets, reserving 0-1 RIR for the very last set only - failure on big barbell lifts is slightly INFERIOR for strength and triples acute neuromuscular fatigue (Robinson/Ruple/Refalo), and on the deadlift it threatens the priority lower-back. Isolation/machine/cable = 0-2 RIR, final set often to failure (cheap fatigue, captures the small hypertrophy premium). At light loads (high-rep cable/calf) train 0-1 RIR so high-threshold units are recruited. Strength-leaning compound work can sit 3-5 RIR and still build strength at matched volume.

PROGRESSION: Double progression - add reps within each exercise's prescribed range, then add load (5-lb barbell steps / next DB) once the top of the range is hit at target RIR. Run ~4-6 week accumulation blocks ramping volume from the low end (~12 sets/muscle) toward the high end (~18-20) and RIR DOWN within the block (e.g. start ~3-4 RIR, finish ~1 RIR) - matched gains at lower average RPE (Martikainen). Then a deload week at ~half volume and 3-6 RIR to shed accumulated fatigue, and restart. The erector/core priority gets the high end of its range and the most consistent weekly load progression because it's painless and trainable - but only while it stays painless (any leg radiation, numbness, give-way, night pain = stop and reassess).

AUTOREGULATION AROUND VARIABLE SPORTS (the lifting is self-sufficient; sports are NOT counted as training volume): The plan is built to stand alone, so on heavy-sport-overlap weeks you AUTOREGULATE the lifting down rather than leaning on the sport for stimulus. Levers, in order: (1) raise RIR by 1-2 on the affected session, (2) drop the last 1-2 accessory sets, (3) on a Tue (pre-kickboxing) or Sat (post-krav) keep big lifts at 2-3 RIR and never to failure. Pull is deliberately scheduled on the kickboxing day (Tue) and the lighter chest-supported Pull after krav (Sat) so leg/push power is fresh for the sport; the heavy fatiguing hinge is isolated to Wed -> Thu rest. If a sport session is unusually brutal, the missing lifting volume is made up later in the week or simply skipped - never added on top into overreaching.

CONSTRAINT INTEGRATION (always on): Shoulder painless-crepitus -> neutral-grip DB / landmine / incline / Smith / machine presses are the press anchors; NO heavy strict barbell overhead press; rotator-cuff + scapular work every Push/Pull. Wrist tendinopathy -> neutral-grip variants, wrist wraps, cable/machine where the wrist stays neutral. Left pinky (temporary) -> FTS cable wrist cuffs / lifting straps / machine-loaded variants on every grip-loaded pull, hinge, and carry; defer crush-grip (gripper/pinch/dead-hang) until it settles. TMJ -> jaw relaxed / mouthguard, no hard clench on any heavy or valsalva set.

EXERCISE-SELECTION LOGIC (from the verified claims): Picks are justified by ROM/muscle-length, stimulus-to-fatigue ratio, and constraint-fit - NOT by EMG/pump 'targeting' (acute EMG and pump do NOT predict regional growth - refuted). Claims I actively USED: standing (gastroc) vs seated (soleus) calf raises; seated leg curl for the hip-extensor hamstring heads; hip-hinge vs leg-curl hamstring split (program BOTH); reclined/extended-hip leg extension for rectus femoris; the modest lengthened-position growth edge (incline curl, lat-prayer, stretch-loaded raises, deep squat). Claims I deliberately did NOT lean on: grip steers lat region, hip-thrust > squat for glute growth, behind-neck press, incline-maximizes-or-combine-for-upper-pec, leg-press/squat-stance muscle reallocation - so the hip thrust is in for the short-length emphasis it adds (not as a squat replacement), and multiple press/pull angles are in for coverage and joint variety (not as proven region-specific growth). Advanced techniques (drop sets, rest-pause, myo-reps, supersets, pyramids) are EQUIVALENT to straight sets at matched volume, so they appear ONLY on the pump days (Fri Push, Sat Pull) purely for time-efficiency and fatigue management - never as a way to claim extra growth. Frequency is the volume vehicle (2x/muscle via PPLx2 satisfies the >=2x floor; beyond that it's logistics), and hypertrophy is load-independent from ~30-85% near failure, which is exactly why each muscle's two weekly sessions are split into one heavier/lower-rep and one lighter/higher-rep pass."

Dynamic mesocycle (the progression)

Length: 10 weeks total = two stacked 4-week accumulation blocks + 1 deload after each (4+1, 4+1). Runs as one continuous mesocycle on top of the static PPL x2 (Sun Legs / Mon Push-heavy / Tue Pull+kickboxing / Wed Legs-heavy-hinge / Thu REST / Fri Push-pump / Sat Pull-lighter+krav). 10 wks is the floor for the Martikainen descending-RIR design (two 5-wk waves incl. deload) and lets the erector/core priority bank ~8 consecutive loaded weeks before the 8-12wk re-check; extend to 12 (three short blocks) only if joints/recovery are still rising at wk10.

Block A · Wk1-2 (MEV entry / re-acclimate) Re-groove every fixed-core compound at the new block's working weights after the prior deload. Hip-hinge erector/core PRIORITY already seeded at the TOP of its range. Fixed-core anchors: Wed heavy hinge = conventional or trap-bar deadlift (trap-bar = lower erector shear, claim regional_bias) cycling with RDL/SLDL; Mon heavy press = incline DB or machine/Smith incline press (shoulder-crepitus: NO heavy strict barbell OHP — constraint.mark.right-shoulder-crepitus); Sun squat = high-bar/front/Smith squat to depth (ml-deep-squat-more-leg-growth). MV ~6 sets/muscle, half the lifts cut. Techniques OFF. 3-6 RIR, no failure. Then re-plan.

Reverse-pyramid (fixed cores): Reverse-pyramid is applied ONLY to the fixed-core compounds (squat, deadlift/hinge, fixed-core incline press, fixed-core row) and ONLY as a loading-organization / time-efficiency tool — it is NOT more growth than equal-volume straight sets (claim.6 'reverse-pyramid more muscle' = equivalent; tech.reverse-pyramid: edge is order/freshness, not hypertrophy). Protocol: after warm-ups, the HEAVIEST top set is performed FIRST while fully fresh (claim.6 rationale: max tension/recruitment on the fresh set, unlike crescent pyramids), then load is dropped ~5-10% per back-off set to extend reps. Concrete on a fixed-core compound: Set1 = top set 4-6 reps @ the strength-leaning RIR for that block week (3-4 RIR early block, 2-3 RIR late; deadlift NEVER below 2-3 RIR — claim.17, rir-strength 3-5 row); Set2 = -7-10% for 6-8 reps @ 2 RIR; Set3 = -7-10% again for 8-10 reps @ 1-2 RIR. Use RPT on at most 1-2 fixed-core lifts per session so total fresh-heavy exposure stays manageable; the rest of the program runs straight sets with double progression (tech.straight-sets). RPT is the heavy-day vehicle (Mon heavy press, Wed heavy hinge, Sun squat); the pump days (Fri/Sat) use straight sets + advanced techniques instead. Load ceilings from the 290 algebra: Olympic bar to ~570 lb and Smith to ~550 give effectively unlimited barbell top-set room; DBs cap at 75 lb (load_algebra_290), so DB fixed-core presses will out-run the top end into higher reps — when a DB lift tops its range at 75 lb, push reps/tempo or migrate that slot to barbell/Smith/machine rather than chasing load. Wrist tendinopathy + left pinky: use neutral grip + FTS cuffs/straps on the heavy top sets (constraint.mark.right-wrist-tendonitis, constraint.mark.left-pinky); TMJ: relaxed jaw / mouthguard on the heavy top set's valsalva (constraint.mark.tmj).

Accessory rotation: Rotate the DYNAMIC-ACCESSORY lifts every block (~every 4-6 wks, at each deload boundary wk5 and wk10) for regional variety, joint health, and stimulus novelty; KEEP the fixed-core anchors stable within a block (only vary their grip/implement). Rotation is justified by joint-variety + ROM/length coverage, NOT by EMG/pump 'targeting' (emg-swelling-predicts-regional-growth = REFUTED). TRICEPS — A overhead-cable-rope-extension (long-head stretch) → B rope-pushdown + skullcrusher. Deload: Cadence: a deload week every 5th week — wk5 and wk10 — i.e. after each 4-week accumulation block (the descending-RIR block ends near the fatigue floor, so deload is scheduled, not reactive). HOW: cut to ~half volume = MV ~6 hard sets/muscle/wk (weekly_sets MV 6 maintenance row; RP deload-to-MV) by dropping sets and removing the back-end accessories, while KEEPING the movement patterns so skill/load is retained. Hold ALL sets at 3-6 RIR with NO sets to failure anywhere (rir-hypertrophy intermediate deload 3-6 row — raising RIR directly removes the fatigue-generating last reps that Refalo showed cost ~3x neuromuscular fatigue and outlast 24h). Loads ~60-70% of the block's top sets. Advanced techniques (drop/rest-pause/myo-reps) OFF during deload (they are high/moderate fatigue_cost, tech rows). The deadlift is deloaded hardest — token light top-set or trap-bar only — to fully unload the priority erectors. EARLY/REACTIVE deload trigger (insert off-schedule): if you can't match the prior week's performance on a lift despite full rest, that's MRV (weekly_sets MRV row) — take that week as a deload or restart at MEV. Each deload also doubles as the autoregulation reset (re-baseline RIR for the next block) and wk10's deload carries the 8-12wk re-check.
