## Philosophy & schedule

RIR TARGETS (evidence-based): Heavy compounds (squat, deadlift, hinge, fixed-core press/pull) = 2-3 RIR most sets, reserving 0-1 RIR for the very last set only - failure on big barbell lifts is slightly INFERIOR for strength and triples acute neuromuscular fatigue (Robinson/Ruple/Refalo), and on the deadlift it threatens the priority lower-back. Isolation/machine/cable = 0-2 RIR, final set often to failure (cheap fatigue, captures the small hypertrophy premium). At light loads (high-rep cable/calf) train 0-1 RIR so high-threshold units are recruited. Strength-leaning compound work can sit 3-5 RIR and still build strength at matched volume.

PROGRESSION: Double progression - add reps within each exercise's prescribed range, then add load (5-lb barbell steps / next DB) once the top of the range is hit at target RIR. Run ~4-6 week accumulation blocks ramping volume from the low end (~12 sets/muscle) toward the high end (~18-20) and RIR DOWN within the block (e.g. start ~3-4 RIR, finish ~1 RIR) - matched gains at lower average RPE (Martikainen). Then a deload week at ~half volume and 3-6 RIR to shed accumulated fatigue, and restart. The erector/core priority gets the high end of its range and the most consistent weekly load progression because it's painless and trainable - but only while it stays painless (any leg radiation, numbness, give-way, night pain = stop and reassess).

AUTOREGULATION AROUND VARIABLE SPORTS (the lifting is self-sufficient; sports are NOT counted as training volume): The plan is built to stand alone, so on heavy-sport-overlap weeks you AUTOREGULATE the lifting down rather than leaning on the sport for stimulus. Levers, in order: (1) raise RIR by 1-2 on the affected session, (2) drop the last 1-2 accessory sets, (3) on a Tue (pre-kickboxing) or Sat (post-krav) keep big lifts at 2-3 RIR and never to failure. Pull is deliberately scheduled on the kickboxing day (Tue) and the lighter chest-supported Pull after krav (Sat) so leg/push power is fresh for the sport; the heavy fatiguing hinge is isolated to Wed -> Thu rest. If a sport session is unusually brutal, the missing lifting volume is made up later in the week or simply skipped - never added on top into overreaching.

CONSTRAINT INTEGRATION (always on): Shoulder painless-crepitus -> neutral-grip DB / landmine / incline / Smith / machine presses are the press anchors; NO heavy strict barbell overhead press; rotator-cuff + scapular work every Push/Pull. Wrist tendinopathy -> neutral-grip variants, wrist wraps, cable/machine where the wrist stays neutral. Left pinky (temporary) -> FTS cable wrist cuffs / lifting straps / machine-loaded variants on every grip-loaded pull, hinge, and carry; defer crush-grip (gripper/pinch/dead-hang) until it settles. TMJ -> jaw relaxed / mouthguard, no hard clench on any heavy or valsalva set.

EXERCISE-SELECTION LOGIC (from the verified claims): Picks are justified by ROM/muscle-length, stimulus-to-fatigue ratio, and constraint-fit - NOT by EMG/pump 'targeting' (acute EMG and pump do NOT predict regional growth - refuted). Claims I actively USED: standing (gastroc) vs seated (soleus) calf raises; seated leg curl for the hip-extensor hamstring heads; hip-hinge vs leg-curl hamstring split (program BOTH); reclined/extended-hip leg extension for rectus femoris; the modest lengthened-position growth edge (incline curl, lat-prayer, stretch-loaded raises, deep squat). Claims I deliberately did NOT lean on: grip steers lat region, hip-thrust > squat for glute growth, behind-neck press, incline-maximizes-or-combine-for-upper-pec, leg-press/squat-stance muscle reallocation - so the hip thrust is in for the short-length emphasis it adds (not as a squat replacement), and multiple press/pull angles are in for coverage and joint variety (not as proven region-specific growth). Advanced techniques (drop sets, rest-pause, myo-reps, supersets, pyramids) are EQUIVALENT to straight sets at matched volume, so they appear ONLY on the pump days (Fri Push, Sat Pull) purely for time-efficiency and fatigue management - never as a way to claim extra growth. Frequency is the volume vehicle (2x/muscle via PPLx2 satisfies the >=2x floor; beyond that it's logistics), and hypertrophy is load-independent from ~30-85% near failure, which is exactly why each muscle's two weekly sessions are split into one heavier/lower-rep and one lighter/higher-rep pass."

**Reverse-pyramid (fixed cores):** Reverse-pyramid is applied ONLY to the fixed-core compounds (squat, deadlift/hinge, fixed-core incline press, fixed-core row) and ONLY as a loading-organization / time-efficiency tool — it is NOT more growth than equal-volume straight sets (claim.6 'reverse-pyramid more muscle' = equivalent; tech.reverse-pyramid: edge is order/freshness, not hypertrophy). Protocol: after warm-ups, the HEAVIEST top set is performed FIRST while fully fresh (claim.6 rationale: max tension/recruitment on the fresh set, unlike crescent pyramids), then load is dropped ~5-10% per back-off set to extend reps. Concrete on a fixed-core compound: Set1 = top set 4-6 reps @ the strength-leaning RIR for that block week (3-4 RIR early block, 2-3 RIR late; deadlift NEVER below 2-3 RIR — claim.17, rir-strength 3-5 row); Set2 = -7-10% for 6-8 reps @ 2 RIR; Set3 = -7-10% again for 8-10 reps @ 1-2 RIR. Use RPT on at most 1-2 fixed-core lifts per session so total fresh-heavy exposure stays manageable; the rest of the program runs straight sets with double progression (tech.straight-sets). RPT is the heavy-day vehicle (Mon heavy press, Wed heavy hinge, Sun squat); the pump days (Fri/Sat) use straight sets + advanced techniques instead. Load ceilings from the 290 algebra: Olympic bar to ~570 lb and Smith to ~550 give effectively unlimited barbell top-set room; DBs cap at 75 lb (load_algebra_290), so DB fixed-core presses will out-run the top end into higher reps — when a DB lift tops its range at 75 lb, push reps/tempo or migrate that slot to barbell/Smith/machine rather than chasing load. Wrist tendinopathy + left pinky: use neutral grip + FTS cuffs/straps on the heavy top sets (constraint.mark.right-wrist-tendonitis, constraint.mark.left-pinky); TMJ: relaxed jaw / mouthguard on the heavy top set's valsalva (constraint.mark.tmj).

**Accessory rotation:** Rotate the DYNAMIC-ACCESSORY lifts every block (~every 4-6 wks, at each deload boundary wk5 and wk10) for regional variety, joint health, and stimulus novelty; KEEP the fixed-core anchors stable within a block (only vary their grip/implement). Rotation is justified by joint-variety + ROM/length coverage, NOT by EMG/pump 'targeting' (emg-swelling-predicts-regional-growth = REFUTED). Pools (all from menu.json, dynamic-accessory role): BICEPS — Block A incline-dumbbell-curl (long-head stretch, mu-lengthened-curl-vs-preacher SUPPORTED) → Block B cable-curl/spider-curl (constant tension, drop-set friendly). TRICEPS — A overhead-cable-rope-extension (long-head stretch) → B rope-pushdown + skullcrusher. HAMSTRINGS split BOTH patterns every block (ml-rdl-hip-dominant-vs-leg-curl-knee-dominant SUPPORTED — program a hinge AND a curl): A = step/dumbbell-RDL + seated-leg-curl (hip-extensor heads at length, ml-seated-vs-lying-leg-curl SUPPORTED) → B = single-leg-RDL/good-morning + lying-leg-curl. QUADS — A leg-extension-extended-hip (reclined RF bias, ml-leg-extension-extended-hip-rectus-femoris SUPPORTED) + Bulgarian split squat → B sissy-squat/reverse-nordic (lengthened RF) + walking-lunge. CALVES — alternate standing (gastroc, ml-standing-vs-seated-calf SUPPORTED) machine/Smith/dumbbell variants block-to-block + ALWAYS keep one seated/bent-knee leg-press calf raise for soleus; add standing-calf lengthened-partials in Block B. DELTS — lateral: A dumbbell/lean-away-lateral-raise → B cable-cuff-lateral-raise (FTS grip-bypass for pinky) / machine-lateral-raise; rear: face-pull ↔ cable-cuff-rear-delt-fly; rotator-cuff + scapular work (cable-external-rotation, full-can-raise, serratus-punch) every Push AND Pull, NON-rotating (constraint.mark.right-shoulder-crepitus). BACK accessories — A chest-supported/incline-bench DB row + wide-grip pulldown → B seated-cable-row-neutral + lat-prayer/straight-arm-pulldown (long-length lat work; mu-row-vs-pulldown bias is only an activation heuristic, not differential growth — REFUTED, so rotate for variety not 'targeting'). GLUTES — hip-thrust variants (machine/Smith/American) ↔ kickbacks + abduction, IN for the short-length emphasis they add, NOT as a squat replacement (ml-hip-thrust-vs-squat-glute-growth REFUTED; ml-hip-thrust-short-length complementary). CORE/ERECTOR (priority, rotates but never drops below its high range): A ab-wheel-rollout (anti-ext) + suitcase-carry (anti-lateral-flexion) + cable-crunch → B weighted-plank/captains-chair-leg-raise + farmers-carry + cable-side-bend; erector loading itself runs through the rotating hinge anchors + good-morning since NO 45-deg/GHD back-ext machine exists (equipment_notes: absent). Grip-loaded accessories (rows, carries, RDLs) ALWAYS use FTS cuffs/straps (constraint.mark.left-pinky); defer crush-grip work (gripper/pinch/dead-hang) until the pinky settles.

**Deload:** Cadence: a deload week every 5th week — wk5 and wk10 — i.e. after each 4-week accumulation block (the descending-RIR block ends near the fatigue floor, so deload is scheduled, not reactive). HOW: cut to ~half volume = MV ~6 hard sets/muscle/wk (weekly_sets MV 6 maintenance row; RP deload-to-MV) by dropping sets and removing the back-end accessories, while KEEPING the movement patterns so skill/load is retained. Hold ALL sets at 3-6 RIR with NO sets to failure anywhere (rir-hypertrophy intermediate deload 3-6 row — raising RIR directly removes the fatigue-generating last reps that Refalo showed cost ~3x neuromuscular fatigue and outlast 24h). Loads ~60-70% of the block's top sets. Advanced techniques (drop/rest-pause/myo-reps) OFF during deload (they are high/moderate fatigue_cost, tech rows). The deadlift is deloaded hardest — token light top-set or trap-bar only — to fully unload the priority erectors. EARLY/REACTIVE deload trigger (insert off-schedule): if you can't match the prior week's performance on a lift despite full rest, that's MRV (weekly_sets MRV row) — take that week as a deload or restart at MEV. Each deload also doubles as the autoregulation reset (re-baseline RIR for the next block) and wk10's deload carries the 8-12wk re-check.

**Autoregulation (for the variable sport load + lower-back):**
- SPORTS ARE NOT TRAINING VOLUME — the lifting stands alone; on heavy-sport-overlap weeks you dial the LIFTING DOWN rather than leaning on the sport for stimulus (philosophy: self-sufficient). Levers, in order: (1) +1-2 RIR on the affected session, (2) drop the last 1-2 accessory sets, (3) keep big lifts at 2-3 RIR and never to failure on sport-adjacent days.
- TUESDAY (Pull, kickboxing PM): Pull is deliberately on the kickboxing day so legs/push power stay fresh for the sport (scheduling_rules). Keep Tue compound rows at 2-3 RIR, never to failure; if kickboxing was brutal last week or you feel pre-fatigued, drop the last 1-2 isolation sets — do NOT add volume on top into overreaching.
- SATURDAY (Pull-lighter, krav AM): Sat is the lighter chest-supported/machine Pull AFTER krav (scheduling_rules: Sat lighter). Run it +1 RIR vs a fresh day, lean on machine/cable + advanced techniques (low skill, low systemic cost). If krav was unusually hard, cut Sat to MV-ish (cut sets) or skip the pump finishers — never make it up by piling onto Sun/Mon.
- WEDNESDAY heavy hinge is islanded before Thursday REST (scheduling_rules) and kept clear of krav/kickboxing days — protect this slot. If the week's sport load was high, hold the hinge at 2-3 RIR and trim accessory hinge volume, but keep the erector/core PRIORITY dose at the high end of its range whenever painless (constraint.mark.lower-back: priority build, not avoidance).
- PICKLEBALL / variable extra sport: treat as added systemic fatigue, not stimulus. If a given week stacks pickleball onto kickboxing+krav, raise RIR by 1 across that week's heavy compounds and let the deload absorb the rest — missing lifting volume is made up later in the week or simply skipped, never added on top.
- WITHIN-BLOCK RIR AUTOREGULATION: ride the descending-RIR wave (start ~3-4 RIR, finish ~1 RIR on non-deadlift compounds and isolation) — matched gains at LOWER average RPE (Martikainen, rir-hypertrophy intermediate descending row). Light high-rep cable/calf 0-1 RIR (load-dependent failure requirement, rir-hypertrophy 0-1 light-load row).
- DEADLIFT / heavy hinge NEVER to momentary failure — 0 RIR ~triples acute neuromuscular fatigue, is slightly inferior for strength, and on the deadlift it threatens the priority lower back (claim.17, claim.18, rir-strength 3-5 row: Robinson/Ruple). Cap it 2-3 RIR every set, all block.
