Philosophy & schedule

FREQUENCY / SPLIT. A 4-day Upper/Lower hits every muscle ~2×/week — the established hypertrophy frequency floor (Schoenfeld 2016: ≥2× beats 1×) and a sufficient strength dose for intermediates — while keeping each session under the ~6–8 quality-set/muscle per-session ceiling (Weightology). That ceiling is exactly why Aaron's high weekly upper volume is split across two upper days rather than crammed into one.

LOADING (the load/rep continuum, honestly). Hypertrophy is load-independent from ~30–85% 1RM at matched effort (Lopez/Schoenfeld — the "heavy load required for hypertrophy" and "failure required" claims are refuted), so most work sits 8–15 reps at moderate load and moderate effort. A genuine 5–8 rep strength anchor per session (one neutral-grip press on each upper day; one knee-dominant and one hip-dominant compound on the lower days) captures the real 1–6 rep / 80–100% 1RM strength-zone specificity. Net mix lands ~60–70% of sets in a personal 6–15 sweet spot, ~15–20% heavier (6–8), ~15–20% lighter (15–20 metabolite work) — the SBS sweet-spot spread.

EFFORT / RIR (autoregulated by exercise class and block week). Compound strength anchors 3–4 RIR early-block → 2–3 RIR late (Helms/Robinson — failure on heavy compounds is slightly inferior for strength and ~triples acute neuromuscular fatigue vs 3 RIR; reserve 0–1 RIR for an end-of-block top set only, and only on the patterns Aaron presses pain-free). Primary hypertrophy compounds 2–3 RIR, last set ~1. Isolation 1–2 RIR, final set to 0–1 (the closer-to-failure hypertrophy premium is cheap on low-systemic-stress lifts; mandatory 0–1 RIR on any light-load high-rep isolation set because motor-unit recruitment is recruitment-late at low loads). RIR ramps down ~4 → ~1 across each 4–5 week accumulation block (Martikainen — equal gains at lower average session-RPE), then a deload at 3–6 RIR.

PROGRESSION. Double-progression within each exercise's rep band: keep load fixed and add reps across sessions until you hit the top of the band on all sets at target RIR, then add the smallest load increment and drop back to the bottom. DBs cap at 75 lb — once a DB lift maxes out, progress via reps / added sets / tempo / structure, never grip-width creep on the shoulder. Within each block, ramp volume from ~MEV (8–12 sets/muscle) into the MAV zone (12–20) by adding ~1–3 sets/week to lagging/priority muscles (lateral delt, rear delt, back, triceps), then deload to MV (~6 sets) when performance stalls and restart at MEV.

SET-STRUCTURE LOGIC (no technique out-grows volume-equated straight sets). Straight sets anchor every shoulder-stakes compound (lowest fatigue cost, best load/technique control) — ~70% of total weekly sets. Time-efficient structures are deployed only where they buy efficiency or fatigue-management without risking the joint: antagonist supersets (Burke 2025 RCT — identical hypertrophy/strength at ~36% less session time, and they conveniently pair scapular pull against pressing); myo-reps and capped machine drop sets on machine/cable isolation only; cluster sets for velocity-maintained heavy pressing; reverse-pyramid as an optional autoregulatory swap on the lower-body anchors only. Pre-exhaust agonist supersets and any high-fatigue technique on overhead/dip/behind-the-body movements are avoided.

CONSTRAINT INTEGRATION (always on). Every press is neutral-grip and front-of-body; the apprehension position (behind-neck press/pulldown, wide end-range overhead) is banned; no deep dips or forced end-range stretch; tempo is controlled and non-ballistic; intensity techniques stay off shoulder-loaded lifts; posterior-girdle volume ≥ pressing volume; the rotator-cuff + scapular block runs twice weekly at full dose as a programmed priority. Red-flag kill switch halts any set on a subluxation/dislocation event, sharp pain, a slipping/instability sensation, arm numbness/tingling, or sudden strength loss — dull working-muscle pump/fatigue is expected and fine.

Upper A — Horizontal-Emphasis (Push-bias) + Shoulder-Health Block #1

Neutral/front-of-body horizontal pressing and rowing; lateral + rear delts; direct biceps/triceps; and the FIRST of two weekly programmed rotator-cuff + scapular-stability blocks. One heavier 6–8 rep neutral-grip press anchors the day; everything else lives 8–20. Posterior-girdle volume (row + rear delt + cuff) exceeds pressing volume per the structural-balance rule. Cuff/scap activation runs first to prime scapular stability and warm the joint, then the big compound press+row, then delts/arms/scap finisher.

No upper-body involvement. [seated knee-flexed raises train the soleus; Lower A's standing raise covers the gastroc]

Keep ribs down, avoid lumbar sag.

Shoulder note (lower day): every implement — RDL bar, Smith hip-thrust/calf bar, side-held dumbbells, ankle-cuff cables, ab wheel — is front-of-body or grip-free with no overhead, behind-neck, deep-dip, or abduction-plus-external-rotation exposure. The apprehension position is fully avoided. The red-flag kill switch still applies: stop and reassess on any subluxation, sharp joint pain, slipping sensation, arm numbness/tingling, or sudden strength loss.

Rest: RDL 3–4 min (heavy hinge anchor); hip thrust / split squat 2–3 min; leg curl / adduction / abduction / calves 60–120 s; ab rollout ~90 s. Antagonist-superset option: pair the seated leg curl (#3) with the Bulgarian split squat (#4) back-to-back for ~36% time savings without losing volume.

Dynamic mesocycle (the progression)

Length: one 4–5 week accumulation block + a deload, run as a continuous mesocycle on top of the static Upper/Lower split. Volume ramps from MEV into the MAV zone while RIR descends ~4 → ~1 (Martikainen — equal gains at lower average session-RPE); the rotator-cuff + scapular block runs at full 12 sets/week from week 1 and never ramps (it is therapeutic baseline, not a progression variable). Run the optional 5th week only if recovery is still tracking; collapse to a 4-week block + deload if performance stalls in week 4.

Week Focus Volume Intensity (RIR)
Wk 1 — Accumulation entry / re-groove Re-groove all four strength anchors (Upper A neutral flat press, Lower A squat, Upper B neutral overhead/incline, Lower B RDL/hip-thrust) with full technique focus; on the shoulder, confirm the neutral/front-of-body press groove is pain-free through full available ROM. Both cuff/scap blocks run at full dose from day 1. Straight-sets only this week (clean reference for the double-progression); introduce antagonist supersets on the non-competing upper pairs once movements feel grooved. START at MEV, biased generous on the therapeutic targets. Roughly: lateral delt ~14, rear delt ~12, back ~14, chest ~11, biceps/triceps ~11 each, quads ~12, hams ~11, glutes ~11, calves ~10, plus the fixed 12-set cuff/scap block at full dose (RULE 8 — therapeutic, not protective under-dosing). Lower body and arms enter at the bottom of their range; shoulder-health and lateral/rear-delt work enter higher because their MEV is effectively their working dose. Top of the descending ramp. Compound anchors ~4 RIR (RPE 6); primary hypertrophy compounds 3 RIR; isolation 2 RIR. Heavy anchors sit high deliberately — failure on big lifts is slightly inferior for strength and ~triples acute neuromuscular fatigue vs 3 RIR.
Wk 2 — Accumulation, first double-progression step On every exercise, attempt the top of its rep band at week-1 load before adding the smallest increment (DBs cap at 75 lb — then reps/added sets/tempo, never grip-width creep on the shoulder). Strength anchors begin loading at the heavy 5–8 end. Antagonist supersets now standard: horizontal-row + neutral-press, rear-delt/face-pull + lateral-raise, biceps + triceps, seated-leg-curl + split-squat. Ramp +1–3 sets into PRIORITY muscles only (lateral delt, rear delt, back, triceps) — not lower body. Target ~ lateral delt 16, rear delt 14, back 16, triceps 12; hold lower body and chest. Cuff/scap stays pinned at 12. Stay inside the 12–20 trained optimum (diminishing returns past ~20). Anchors ~3–4 RIR; primary hypertrophy compounds 2–3 RIR; isolation 1–2 RIR with the last set of each isolation to 0–1; any light-load high-rep isolation set to 0–1 (recruitment is recruitment-late at low loads).
Wk 3 — Accumulation peak / push toward MAV Continue double progression — most exercises now adding load or sitting mid-band after last week's add. Bring the remaining muscles up to target. Introduce time-efficient intensity structures ONLY on machine/cable isolation: myo-reps or one capped drop-set on lateral raise / leg extension / calf / triceps pushdown — kept OFF every shoulder-loaded and free-weight lift (RULE 5). Optional cluster sets on the heavy neutral-grip press for a top-end strength bias (velocity-maintained, lower fatigue, keeps technique crisp on the unstable shoulder). Near top of the MAV climb: lateral delt ~18, rear delt ~16, back ~18 (the generous therapeutic ceilings), chest ~13–14, quads ~14–15, hams ~13–14, glutes ~13–14, arms ~13–14, calves ~12. Brushes the upper end of the 12–20 optimum — do NOT exceed absent a clear MRV headroom signal. Cuff/scap remains pinned at 12. Anchors ~3 RIR; primary hypertrophy compounds ~2 RIR, last set ~1; isolation 1 RIR, final sets to 0–1 (0 on light-load high-rep finishers). RIR continuing its descent.
Wk 4 — Intensification (effort over added volume) Volume HOLDS at the week-3 ceiling; the progression lever shifts to LOAD on the strength anchors and proximity-to-failure on hypertrophy work. The genuine 5–8 strength zone is expressed: anchors push load at the heavy end, optionally via reverse-pyramid on the LOWER-body anchors only (heaviest top set first while fresh; AVOIDED on overhead/unstable-shoulder pressing where a warmed-up straight-set ramp is safer). Watch for MRV signals and hold any muscle that flags one. HOLD volume at week-3 levels; redirect adaptation pressure into intensity, not more sets. If a muscle missed performance vs week 3, drop IT back ~2–3 sets while leaving the others — never a blanket cut. If fatigue forces a trim, the cuff/scap block and lateral/rear-delt therapeutic volume are the LAST things touched (RULE 8). Anchors ~2–3 RIR (reserve 0–1 for an end-of-block top set, and only on lower-body / neutral-press lifts that don't compromise the joint); primary hypertrophy compounds ~1–2 RIR, last set failure-adjacent; isolation 0–1 throughout.
Wk 5 — Intensification peak (optional) Run it only if recovery is still tracking. Final realization: top-end loads on the four anchors at their lowest planned RIR, hypertrophy work at peak proximity. No new structures — an expression week, so shoulder-stakes lifts stay on plain straight-sets/clusters at controlled non-ballistic tempo (RULE 6). End-of-block top sets are the ONLY place 0–1 RIR is permitted on a heavy press, and even then only the neutral/front patterns Aaron presses pain-free. Still HELD at the block ceiling (lateral delt ~18, rear delt ~16, back ~18, etc.) — a brief touch of MRV to trigger supercompensation, not a new volume PR. Expect most muscles to finally signal MRV (can't beat prior performance) — that's the trigger to deload, not to add sets. Bottom of the ramp (~RIR 1 average). Anchors ~2 RIR (a single end-of-block top set may touch 0–1 on safe patterns only); primary hypertrophy compounds ~1; isolation 0–1 / final sets to failure. 1–3 RIR already captures nearly the full hypertrophy stimulus — do not grind every set to 0.
Deload (wk 6 after a 5-wk block; wk 5 after a 4-wk block) A fatigue flush, not a layoff — keep the movement patterns and the joint moving (avoiding the sedentary state that flares the shoulder); just drop the dose. TWO LEVERS, RIR FIRST. (1) Raise effort to 3–6 RIR on everything (the higher-value, lower-cost lever — removes the fatigue-generating last reps). (2) Cut volume toward MV (~6 sets/muscle) on the high-fatigue work: heavy lower-body anchors (squat/RDL/hip-thrust) and the heaviest pressing, halved or dropped to ~6 sets, loads pulled back ~10–20%, reps submaximal. CRITICAL ASYMMETRY — protect the therapeutic upper dose: do NOT cut the upper body to MV uniformly. The cuff/scap block stays at/near its full 12 sets (low-fatigue, never to grinding failure, the literal mechanism that resolves Aaron's symptoms). Lateral/rear-delt and back work drop only modestly (~8–10 sets), not to 6. After the deload, restart at MEV with RIR ~4 and re-run the ramp. 3–6 RIR, no failure anywhere. The deload is a fatigue-management tool, not an independent growth driver — its job is to let the next block start fresh.

Autoregulation & how to read this

Day-to-day / week-to-week adjustments — ordered so upper-body therapeutic volume is the LAST thing cut, never the first.

  • RED-FLAG KILL SWITCH (overrides everything, RULE: stop-and-reassess). STOP the set and reassess immediately on any of — a dislocation/subluxation event, sharp pain, a sense of the shoulder slipping/instability, numbness or tingling down the arm, or sudden loss of strength. These are joint-line/neural signals, categorically different from the dull working-muscle fatigue/pump that is expected and fine. A red flag ends that exercise for the day; it does NOT mean cut the week's upper-body volume.
  • WHEN THE SHOULDER FEELS TIGHT (but no red flag). This is a cue to MODIFY THE MOVEMENT, NOT remove volume — in-ROM upper training is what resolves Aaron's symptoms (RULE 8). In priority order: (1) shrink ROM to the pain-free/stable portion and keep the set; (2) swap the loaded pattern for a friendlier one in the same muscle target — neutral-grip / front-of-body / machine or cable instead of free-weight, landmine instead of straight-bar overhead (RULE 1); (3) slow the tempo and drop the load, keeping 2–3 RIR (RULE 4, no ballistic reps); (4) front-load that day's cuff + scap block as a "feeder" to warm and stabilize the joint, then re-attempt the pressing. Only AFTER these fail do you reduce that single exercise's sets — and you bank the removed sets into the cuff/scap or rear-delt work, keeping the girdle balanced (RULE 7).
  • WHEN RECOVERY IS POOR (bad sleep, high life stress, can't match last session's reps/load at adequate rest = an MRV signal). CUT EFFORT BEFORE VOLUME, AND CUT THE RIGHT VOLUME FIRST. (1) First lever — raise RIR by ~1–2 across the session (anchors to 3–4, isolation to 2); this recovers cheaply and 1–3 RIR already captures nearly all the hypertrophy stimulus. (2) Second lever — if still flat, trim sets from the HIGHEST-FATIGUE work FIRST: the heavy lower-body anchors and the heaviest pressing, and drop the optional intensity techniques (drop-sets/myo-reps) before anything else. (3) PROTECTED LAST — the cuff/scap block, lateral/rear-delt, and back volume are the final things touched; the cuff/scap block is effectively never cut on a bad week (low-fatigue, therapeutic). A bad week is exactly when the sedentary-flare risk is highest, so the dose that keeps the shoulder healthy must survive.
  • WHEN PERFORMANCE STALLS ACROSS A WHOLE MUSCLE for 2+ sessions despite rest: that muscle has hit MRV — hold or shave 2–3 sets from THAT muscle only (not a blanket cut), or trigger the deload if multiple muscles flag together.
  • DEFAULT STRUCTURE BIAS UNDER FATIGUE: when time or recovery is short, keep the shoulder-stakes compounds on plain straight-sets (lowest fatigue, best load/technique control, and the structure no advanced technique out-grows at matched volume) and shed the time-saving structures rather than the safe ones. Use antagonist supersets to preserve total volume in less time on a rushed day BEFORE cutting any sets.

How to read the tables. - Sets × Reps is the working prescription after warm-ups (warm-up ramps are not counted as working volume). - RIR = Reps In Reserve at the target point in the block; the early-block / late-block split shows the descending ramp (e.g. "3 early → 1–2 late"). - Load is a conservative week-1 starting point and the progression rule for that lift. DBs cap at 75 lb — once a DB lift tops its band at 75 on all sets, progress via reps / tempo / added set / structure, not load; barbell and Smith are effectively uncapped at this gym. - Structure names the set-structure used and (where relevant) which exercise it supersets with. Straight-sets is the default and anchors every shoulder-stakes compound; intensity techniques (drop / myo-reps / cluster) appear only where the Notes permit, and never on a shoulder-loaded press. - Notes / accommodation carries the cue, the shoulder rule encoded, and the inline citation justifying the choice.
