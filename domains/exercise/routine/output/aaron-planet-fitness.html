How to read this / autoregulation

RIR targets (evidence-based). Compound strength anchors (the one heavier 5–8 rep neutral-grip press per upper day, the squat, the RDL/hinge) sit 3–4 RIR early-block, intensifying to 2–3 RIR late-block — failure on big lifts is slightly inferior for strength and roughly triples acute neuromuscular fatigue vs ~3 RIR (exercise.claim.17), so 0–1 RIR is reserved for an end-of-block top set on safe patterns only. Primary hypertrophy compounds (rows, secondary presses, leg press, hinge accessories) sit 2–3 RIR, last set ~1. Isolation (lateral/rear delts, curls, extensions, calves, leg curl, leg extension) runs 1–2 RIR with the final set of each exercise to 0–1 RIR — a cheap place to spend the closer-to-failure premium (exercise.claim.18); any light-load, high-rep isolation set must reach 0–1 RIR because motor-unit recruitment is recruitment-late at low loads.

Progression — double-progression + RIR autoregulation across a 4–5 week accumulation block, then deload. Add reps within each exercise's prescribed band at target RIR; once you hit the top of the band on all sets, add the smallest load increment and drop back to the bottom. Dumbbells cap at 80 lb at this gym — once a DB lift maxes out, progress via reps / added sets / tempo, never grip-width creep. Ramp volume from MEV (~8–12 sets/muscle) up through the MAV zone toward MRV across the block, biasing the therapeutic targets (lateral delt, rear delt, back, cuff/scap) to the generous end; ramp RIR down from ~4 to ~1 across the block (Martikainen — equal gains at lower average session-RPE); then deload to ~MV and restart at MEV.

Set-structure logic (from the corpus's central refutation). NO advanced technique out-grows volume-equated straight sets (exercise.claim.13), so straight sets anchor every shoulder-stakes compound (lowest fatigue, best load/technique control) and carry ~70% of weekly sets. Time-efficient structures are deployed only where they buy efficiency without risking the joint: antagonist supersets on non-competing upper pairs and the leg-curl/leg-extension pair (Burke 2025 RCT — equal growth, ~36% less session time); myo-reps and a single capped drop-set on machine/cable isolation only; cluster sets optional on the heavy neutral-grip press for a velocity-maintained top-end strength bias.

Autoregulation — ordered so upper-body therapeutic volume is the LAST thing cut.

  1. RED-FLAG KILL SWITCH (overrides everything; RULE 9): STOP the set and reassess on any of — a dislocation/subluxation event, sharp pain, a sense of the shoulder slipping/instability, numbness or tingling down the arm, or sudden loss of strength. These are joint-line/neural signals, categorically different from the dull working-muscle pump that is expected and fine. A red flag ends that exercise for the day; it does not mean cut the week's upper-body volume.
  2. Shoulder feels TIGHT (no red flag) → MODIFY the movement, do NOT remove volume. In order: (a) shrink ROM to the stable/pain-free portion and keep the set; (b) swap to a friendlier pattern in the same target (neutral-grip / machine / cable instead of free-weight); (c) slow tempo and drop load, keeping 2–3 RIR; (d) front-load that day's cuff/scap block + face-pulls as a feeder to warm and stabilize the joint, then re-attempt the press. Only after these fail do you trim that single exercise's sets — and you bank the removed sets into cuff/scap or rear-delt work (RULE 8).
  3. Recovery poor (bad sleep/stress, can't match last session = MRV signal) → cut effort before volume, and cut the right volume first. Raise RIR ~1–2 across the session; if still flat, trim sets from the highest-fatigue work first (heavy lower-body anchors, heaviest pressing) and drop the optional intensity techniques. The cuff/scap block, lateral/rear-delt, and back volume are the protected last things touched.
  4. Whole-muscle stall for 2+ sessions = that muscle hit MRV: hold or shave 2–3 sets from THAT muscle only (not a blanket cut), or deload if several flag together.

The 4 sessions

Upper A — Horizontal-Emphasis (Push-bias) + Programmed Shoulder-Health Block #1

Neutral-grip / front-of-body horizontal pressing and rowing, lateral + rear delts, direct arms, and the FIRST of two weekly rotator-cuff + scapular-stability blocks. One heavier 5–8 neutral-grip press is the strength anchor; everything else 8–15. Posterior-girdle (row + rear delt + cuff) volume is held ≥ pressing volume, and the cuff/scap work is a programmed priority. All pressing stays neutral-grip, front-of-body, controlled tempo, full-but-pain-free ROM — no apprehension position, no deep dips, no behind-the-neck, no forced end-range stretch.

Structure & rest. Run three antagonist supersets to preserve volume in less time (Burke 2025, ~36% time saved) — A2+B (press/row), E+D (face-pull/lateral-raise), G+H (curl/pushdown) — but treat the heavy DB press (#1) as plain straight sets first while fresh. Rest: ~3 min on the heavy DB press · ~90–120 s shared on each antagonist superset · ~60–90 s on isolation · ~45–75 s on the cuff/scap work (never to grinding failure). Loads are week-1 entry references at the higher-RIR end of the descending block ramp.

Shoulder call-out (Upper A). KILL SWITCH (RULE 9): stop and reassess on any subluxation/slip sensation, sharp joint-line pain, arm numbness/tingling, or sudden strength loss — a dull muscle pump is fine. If the shoulder feels tight (no red flag): shrink ROM to the stable portion, slow tempo, or front-load the external-rotation + serratus work as a feeder, then re-attempt the press — modify the movement, do NOT cut the upper-body volume. The cuff/scap block (orders 5–7) is the literal mechanism that resolves Aaron's symptoms — it runs at full dose from week 1 and is the last thing ever trimmed.

3 Leg Press 3 × 10–15 1–2 ~3–4 plates/side (~315–405 lb incl. sled); high/wide foot placement to bias glute/ham on this hip day antagonist-superset (pair with #2) PF machine the home setup lacks — secondary quad volume + glute with zero spinal load, sparing the RDL-fatigued erectors; superset with the leg curl.
4 Barbell Hip Thrust 3 × 10–15 1–2 barbell ~225 lb (start ~135–185, climb); pad the bar straight-sets Highest-EMG bilateral glute loader at the SHORT/peak-contracted length — complements the long-length stretch the RDL/squat give the glutes; spine-friendly, drive through heels to a hard lockout.
5 Hip Adduction Machine 3 × 12–20 0–1 (final set) machine ~mid stack; open pads to a deep abducted stretch for full ROM straight-sets Exploits a PF machine the home gym lacks; high-SFR adductor volume (~half the weekly 8 sets) loaded into the lengthened position.
6 Seated Calf Raise (machine) 3 × 15–30 0–1 machine ~mid stack; full stretch at the bottom, pause at top straight-sets; myo-reps optional when time-limited Knee-FLEXED seated position targets the soleus — the head Lower A's standing/knee-extended work skips — so the gastroc/soleus split is covered across the week.
7 Ab-Wheel Rollout 3 × 8–15 1–2 bodyweight (shorten ROM to scale; kneeling start) straight-sets Anti-extension core to balance the day's heavy hinge loading on the erectors/QL; high-SFR, lengthened-range bias. Keep the rollout within a stable shoulder range (no overhead end-range stress).

Structure & rest. Straight-sets/double-progression on the RDL anchor, hip thrust, and leg press (the lowest-fatigue benchmark, ~70% of sets). Antagonist superset on seated leg curl + leg press (Burke 2025 RCT — same growth, ~36% less time; knee-flexor hamstrings rest while quads/glutes work). Myo-reps available on the seated calf finisher when time-limited. Hamstrings get BOTH a hip hinge (RDL — hip-extensor heads at long length) and a knee-flexion curl (seated — semitendinosus/distal BF). Calf head split: Lower A = standing/gastroc, Lower B = seated/soleus (Kinoshita). DB cap is 80 lb here, so the barbell RDL (not DB) is the correct heavy hinge anchor; erector-spinae fatigue from the RDL is the practical ceiling on the day, which is why the spine-offloaded hip thrust and leg press follow it rather than a second free-weight hinge. Rests: 3–4 min on the RDL, 2–3 min on leg press/hip thrust, 60–120 s on isolation.

Shoulder call-out (Lower B). A shoulder-neutral day — all loading is front-of-body or machine, nothing touches the apprehension position, so the joint just gets a low-stress hinge grip (no overhead / end-range ER anywhere). The RDL grip is double-overhand and front-of-body; the ab-wheel rollout is kept within a stable shoulder range.

Mesocycle (4–5 week accumulation block → deload, then restart at MEV)

Volume ramps MEV → MAV across the block, biased to the therapeutic upper targets; RIR ramps down from ~4 to ~1 (Martikainen — equal gains at lower average session-RPE). The rotator-cuff + scapular block runs at its full 12 sets/week from week 1 — it is a therapeutic constant, never a ramp variable.

Week Focus RIR Volume
1 — Accumulation entry / re-groove Bed in bar path on the four anchors (DB flat press, squat, machine overhead, RDL) at heavy 5–8 but LIGHT effort; confirm the neutral/front press groove is pain-free through full available ROM. Straight-sets only this week so every load/RIR is a clean reference. Both cuff/scap blocks already at full dose. Anchors ~4 (RPE 6); primary compounds 3; isolation 2 START at MEV, generous for therapeutic upper targets: lateral delt ~14, rear delt ~12, back ~14, chest ~11, biceps/triceps ~11 each, quads ~12, hams ~11, glutes ~11, calves ~10 — plus the fixed 12-set cuff/scap block (does NOT start low).
2 — First double-progression step Attempt the top of each rep band at week-1 load before adding the smallest increment (DBs cap at 80 lb). Anchors begin loading at the heavy 5–8 end. Antagonist supersets now standard (row+press, face-pull+lateral-raise, biceps+triceps, leg-curl+leg-press). Anchors ~3–4; primary compounds 2–3; isolation 1–2, last set of each to 0–1 RAMP +1–3 sets into PRIORITY muscles only: lateral delt ~16, rear delt ~14, back ~16, triceps ~12; hold lower body and chest. Cuff/scap stays pinned at 12.
3 — Accumulation peak / push toward MAV Most lifts now adding load or sitting mid-band. Bring lower body, chest, biceps up to the upper-middle of their range. Introduce time-efficient intensity structures ONLY on machine/cable isolation (myo-reps or one capped drop-set on lateral raise / leg extension / calf / pushdown) — kept OFF every shoulder-loaded lift (RULE 7). Optional clusters on the heavy machine press. Anchors ~3; primary compounds ~2 (last set ~1); isolation 1, finals to 0–1 Near top of the MAV climb: lateral delt ~18, rear delt ~16, back ~18, chest ~13–14, quads ~14–15, hams ~13–14, glutes ~13–14, arms ~13–14, calves ~12. Cuff/scap still at 12.
4 — Intensification (effort over added volume) Volume HOLDS at the week-3 ceiling; the lever shifts to LOAD on the anchors and proximity-to-failure on hypertrophy work. Genuine 5–8 strength zone expressed; optional reverse-pyramid on the LOWER-body anchors only (AVOIDED on overhead/unstable-shoulder pressing). Hold any muscle that flags an MRV signal. Anchors ~2–3 (0–1 only on safe lower/neutral-press lifts); primary compounds ~1–2; isolation 0–1 HOLD at week-3 levels; redirect adaptation into intensity, not sets. If a muscle missed performance, drop IT ~2–3 sets only. The cuff/scap and lateral/rear-delt volume are the LAST things touched.
5 — Intensification peak (optional) Run it only if recovery still tracks (else collapse to a 4-week block + deload). Top-end loads on the four anchors at lowest planned RIR; no new structures — shoulder-stakes lifts stay on plain straight-sets/clusters at controlled tempo (RULE 6). End-of-block top sets are the ONLY place 0–1 RIR is permitted on a heavy press, and only on the neutral/front patterns Aaron presses pain-free. Anchors ~2 (a single top set may touch 0–1 on safe patterns only); primary compounds ~1; isolation 0–1 / finals to failure Still HELD at the block ceiling — a brief touch of MRV to trigger supercompensation, not a new volume PR.
Deload (week 5 or 6) Fatigue flush, not a layoff (sedentariness flares the shoulder). RIR FIRST: raise effort to 3–6 RIR on everything. Then cut volume toward MV (~6 sets) on the high-fatigue work only — heavy lower-body anchors + heaviest pressing halved, loads −10–20%. CRITICAL ASYMMETRY: do NOT cut the upper body to MV uniformly — the cuff/scap block stays at/near its full 12 sets (low-fatigue, the literal mechanism that resolves the symptoms); lateral/rear-delt and back drop only modestly (~8–10 sets). After deload, restart the next mesocycle at MEV with RIR back at ~4. 3–6 RIR everywhere High-fatigue compounds → ~MV (6 sets); cuff/scap held ~12; lateral/rear-delt/back ~8–10.
